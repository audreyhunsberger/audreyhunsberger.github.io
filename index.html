Data report · Jan 2025 – Aug 2026

ICE by the Numbers

"ICE by the Numbers" combines the agency's required detention statistics with individual-level FOIA records from the Deportation Data Project, covering January 2025 through August 2026.

Read the full report View as PDF
  • ICE reports that 60% of people in detention have a "criminal record," but that figure lumps convictions together with pending charges. Only 29.4% of those detained under the second Trump administration had a conviction of any kind.
  • The share of people in detention with no criminal record or pending charges has steadily decreased since the beginning of the second Trump administration, from 47.9% in January 2025 to 23.7% in August 2026.
  • 6,142 minors have been detained since January 2025, including 1,913 children under the age of 10.
  • The average bond amount for a person booked into ICE custody increased from $6,910 in February 2025 to $9,539 in July 2026.
  • ICE's FY2026 data had to add a category for court-ordered releases. In June 2026, 1,853 detainees were released under a court order.
  • ICE's official data disclosed ten U.S. citizens arrested across FY2025 and FY2026; however, independent reporting by ProPublica found 170 such cases from January to October 2025 alone.

Sources: ICE detention statistics (FY2025–FY2026) and individual-level FOIA records from the Deportation Data Project. U.S. citizen arrest count from ProPublica reporting.
